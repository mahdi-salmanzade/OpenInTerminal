Use Open in Terminal

In Finder, select one or more folders and right-click. Choose Services > Open in Terminal, or look in Quick Actions.

If the action is missing, look for “Open in Terminal” in System Settings (or System Preferences) > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts > Services, and enable it. You may need to log out and log back in once for macOS to discover the service.

Each selected folder opens in Apple's Terminal. The exact window or tab behavior follows Terminal's settings.

To uninstall, remove /Library/Services/Open in Terminal.workflow. Other services and Terminal settings are unaffected.
