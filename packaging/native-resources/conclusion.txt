One more step: enable the Finder extension

1. Open “Open in Terminal” from Applications.
2. Click “Enable Finder Extension” and turn on Open in Terminal in the settings pane.
3. Right-click a folder in Finder and look for Open in Terminal and Open in New Terminal Window in the main menu.

macOS may ask to allow Open in Terminal to control Terminal when you first use the new-window action. The ordinary action does not require this automation permission.

If you installed the earlier Services-only version, remove its “Open in Terminal.workflow” from /Library/Services or ~/Library/Services to avoid keeping the old submenu entry.

To uninstall, disable the extension and move Open in Terminal.app from Applications to the Trash. No login item or background daemon is installed.
